# Claude Acaba de CAMBIAR: Pizarra, Data y Apps en el Chat

https://youtube.com/watch?v=WLOBEbXKeN0&si=UXU_x_TkC-qYmBL8

## Resumen

This video covers four major updates released by Claude and how to implement them, using a hypothetical restaurant business as a testing scenario.

Key Novelties

Pizarra (Whiteboard): Allows users to convert disorganized notes or project plans into visual layouts (01:52 - 06:10).
Hojas (Sheets): Enables the creation of spreadsheets directly from natural language prompts, though availability is currently limited (06:10 - 06:42).
Data: Replaces the former Dashboards tool, allowing users to turn CSV data into interactive panels or story-like reports without needing external database connectors (06:42 - 10:44).
Apps within the Chat: This feature allows third-party apps like Notion or Adobe to open directly inside Claude. The video demonstrates installing three official MCP (Model Context Protocol) apps into Claude Desktop without needing complex server setups or coding (10:44 - 14:52).

Access & Availability

General Access: Pizarra, Hojas, and Data are being rolled out to all paid subscribers on web, desktop, and mobile, though they are in various stages of beta rollout (00:33 - 01:27).
Claude Meetings: A newer feature for Team and Enterprise plans, enabling Claude to join Google Meet calls for live note-taking (01:10).

Expert Verdict

While tools like Pizarra and Data are stable enough for immediate use, the speaker advises caution with custom-built internal apps, noting that the ecosystem is still maturing (14:52 - 15:49).
